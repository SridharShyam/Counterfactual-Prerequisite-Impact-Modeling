This document provides IEEE-format bibliographic entries for the exact 14 references cited in the manuscript (`[1]` through `[14]`). Each entry is audited directly against the documentation, benchmark tables, and audit logs existing in the repository (`reports/tables/table_literature_benchmark.csv`, `reports/tables/table_literature_benchmark_FINAL.csv`, `reports/tables/ieee_tables.tex`, `reports/IEEE_COMPARATIVE_EVALUATION.md`, and `reports/issue_log.md`).

## Audit Standard & Integrity Rule

- **VERIFIED IN REPOSITORY:** Sourced directly from verifiable entries in repository reports, benchmark tables, or conference audit records for that specific paper. Bibliographic details (authors, title, venue, year, pages/DOI/URL) are provided exactly as evidenced without embellishment.
- **FLAGGED / VENUE DISCREPANCY:** Bibliographic details exist in the repository but require explicit author reconciliation (e.g., conflicting proceedings venue or errant DOI cataloged during audits).
- **INCOMPLETE — not found in repository; requires author verification against the original source:** Stated whenever full bibliographic details (publication venue, year, volume, page numbers, or official DOI) do not exist in the repository for that specific paper. No bibliographic data is fabricated.
