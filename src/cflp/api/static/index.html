Data Governance & Educational Privacy Policy

1. Dataset Privacy & De-identification: The research prototype uses anonymized sequence interactions from the open-source XES3G5M dataset. All student identifiers (e.g. uid) are pseudonymous integers devoid of Personally Identifiable Information (PII), biometric identifiers, or institutional affiliations.

2. In-Memory Processing: Student interaction queries submitted through the REST API are evaluated transiently in memory for knowledge-state estimation and are not stored in persistent third-party databases.

3. Research Attribution: The English-translated Knowledge Component metadata is provided solely for academic evaluation and reproducibility under educational open-research standards.

Research Use Guidelines & Limitations

1. Scope of Utility: This software is an engineering research prototype designed to evaluate Graph Neural Network message-passing sensitivity under hypothetical state interventions. It is intended for peer review, scientific evaluation, and educational curriculum research.

2. Non-Clinical / Non-Diagnostic Warning: Impact rankings and generated learning paths do not constitute certified psychological or educational clinical assessments. Real classroom deployments require validation by certified educational practitioners.

3. Open Science Commitment: Code, model checkpoints, and deterministic audit reports are provided to ensure full transparency and peer reproducibility.
